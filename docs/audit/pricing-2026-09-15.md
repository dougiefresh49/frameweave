## Worked example: 30-minute video, 60 frames at 1280x720
- Frames to gemini-2.5-flash-lite: 60 × 516 = 31k image tokens + ~3k prompt ≈ $0.0034 in + ~3k out ≈ $0.0012 → ≈ $0.005 (≈ $0.0023 batch).
- Frames to gpt-5-nano at low detail: 60 × 85 = 5.1k tokens ≈ $0.0003 in; at high detail cost unknown until the calculator is run.
- Transcription: local whisper $0; Groq $0.02; gpt-transcribe $0.135; whisper-1 $0.18.
- Whole-video gemini-3.6-flash at high: 1800 s × 290 = 522k tokens ≈ $0.39 in + output ≈ $0.45.

## YouTube platform facts (scout, from yt-dlp wiki and issue tracker)
- PO Token enforcement expanding through 2026; yt-dlp wiki (edited 2026-07-12) recommends a PO Token Provider plugin (e.g. bgutil-ytdlp-pot-provider) feeding the mweb client; tokens are bound per video id.
- A separate android_vr client regression caused widespread 403s in stable releases up to 2026.07.04, fixed in 2026.08.19. Pin ≥ 2026.08.19 or force player_client=android as a stopgap.
- Captions (manual and auto) are a separate TimedText resource and can be fetched without downloading media (what youtube-transcript-api does); unofficial endpoint, datacenter IPs get blocked more readily.
